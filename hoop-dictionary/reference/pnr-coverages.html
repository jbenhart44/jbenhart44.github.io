Pick-and-roll coverages

How two defenders handle a ball screen. This decides a team’s defensive identity.

All families · 9 terms · 0 full, 0 partial, 9 short

At the level

The big meets the handler at the level of the screen, between drop and hedge.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Drop coverage, Hedge

Blitz

Also: trap

Both defenders trap the handler at the screen, leaving three defenders against four.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Short roll, 4-on-3, Hedge

Drop coverage

Also: drop

Against a ball screen the big stays back near the paint while the guard fights over the screen.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Pick-and-roll, Snake dribble, Ice

Hedge

Also: show, hard hedge

The big jumps out above the screen to stop the handler, then recovers to the roller.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Drop coverage, Blitz

Ice

Also: blue, down, push

On a side ball screen the on-ball defender forces the handler toward the sideline and away from the screen, with the big dropped toward the baseline.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Drop coverage, Step-up screen

Man-to-man

Each defender is responsible for one attacker wherever he goes.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Zone defense, Switch

Next

Also: next switch, up-to-touch

A switch called by the big on a ball screen where the guard’s defender stays attached until the big has the handler.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.5 · See also Switch

Switch

The two defenders in a screen trade assignments.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.5 · See also Scram switch, Mismatch, Peel switch

Under

Also: go under

The on-ball defender goes beneath the screen, conceding the pull-up to keep the drive in front.

defense · Half court · short · Taught in lecture 4.3 · See also Drop coverage
