Shot zones

  • At the rim: within 4 feet of the basket (the restricted area).
  • Paint: the rest of the painted lane.
  • Midrange: any other two-point shot.
  • Corner three: a three from the corner, 22 feet out.
  • Above-the-break three: any other three, 23 feet 9 inches out.
