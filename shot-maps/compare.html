Pick any two player-seasons, from any two seasons, and they sit side by side: the map, the advanced stats and the shot types, the same as on the season pages. Each map's color is against the league in that player's own season, so two seasons compare as each player against his own league. Shot-type labels changed over the years, so shot types compare cleanly only within one season. Hover or tap a hexagon for its numbers.

Percentage points above or below the league: -15-10-5+0+5+10+15

In the pooled view a hexagon's color comes from it and the six around it, but its size is still his own attempts in that one hexagon, so a small hexagon can carry a strong color its neighbours earned. Hover or tap it to see how many shots the color rests on.
