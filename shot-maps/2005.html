The 20 leading scorers by points per game among players who played at least 70 games or scored at least 1,400 points, the season's scoring-title rule. 2 of the 20 qualified by points rather than games: Kobe Bryant, Corey Maggette. Each hexagon is a patch of the half court 5 feet across. Size is how often the player shot from it (full size at 50 or more attempts). Color is his field-goal percentage from it and the six hexagons around it, minus the whole league's from the same seven: orange is better than the league, blue is worse, and a hexagon is colored where he took at least 10 shots across the seven. Hover a hexagon for its numbers, or tap it on a phone and they appear under the map.

Percentage points above or below league average: -15%-10%-5%+0%+5%+10%+15%

Size is about attempts and color is about efficiency.

Under each map are his season's advanced stats from Basketball-Reference's regular-season table, retrieved 2026-09-27; open "All advanced stats" for the rest, and see what PER, Win Shares, BPM and VORP mean. The map shows where and how well he shot. PER, Win Shares, BPM and VORP are Basketball-Reference's own estimates of all-around value: the map does not measure them, and this site has no second source to check them against. eFG% is the exception: it is also recomputed from the shot data, and the two agree (check G-E9 below).

Then his shot types, as NBA.com labelled them: attempts, his field-goal percentage, and the league's with the same type. This season NBA.com used 29 labels, and "Jump Shot" is 59.6% of the league's attempts. The labels changed over the years (see the how this site works), so compare shot types within a season, not across seasons.

What the advanced stats mean

Each definition is Basketball-Reference's own, taken from the column headers of the page the numbers come from. For PER, Win Shares, BPM and VORP in more depth, see this page.

eFG%: Effective Field Goal Percentage
This statistic adjusts for the fact that a 3-point field goal is worth one more point than a 2-point field goal.
